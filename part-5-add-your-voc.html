Add Your VOC

VOC means voice of customer: the actual words your buyers use. Claude goes and finds them, so your copy stops sounding like marketing.
Uses Plan mode About 30 minutes of your time Claude then works alone for hours

You can write a perfect ad that nobody responds to, because it's in your words and they don't talk like that.

When I did this, Claude pulled over 4,600 real quotes, and some of the words I'd been using all the time were ones my buyers hardly ever say. Yours will have their own words, and you can't guess them. Small swaps are the difference between someone thinking "that's me" and scrolling past.

This is your research

Claude goes and finds your buyers, wherever they talk. Your bank will look nothing like mine, even if you sell the same thing, because your audience is different. Don't copy anyone else's words into your files.

You'll end up with two new files. A big language bank holds every quote, sorted by theme, with where it came from. A short voice-of-customer.md joins your foundation files, so Claude has your buyers' words in every session.

The Steps

1

Switch to Plan mode and paste this

Research how they talkHere's what I want to end up with: a bank of real quotes showing how my ideal client talks about their problems, what they want and what they've tried, in their own words. Read my foundation files first. Then tell me how you'd find these people talking to each other online: forums, podcasts, reviews, YouTube comments, blogs and anywhere else they really hang out. Include anyone else who influences their decision. Rules for the research: - Every quote is copied as closely as you can, with who said it, roughly when, and a link. - Save everything as you go in PROJECTS/ICP/voc, with one file per type of source, so nothing's lost if you stop. - Public pages only. No logging in, signing up, joining groups, posting or getting round bot checks. If a site blocks you, note it and move on. - Tell me what you couldn't reach, so I can get it another way. Show me the plan before you start.
2

Check the plan's looking in the right places

You know where your people hang out better than Claude does. Add the forums, groups, podcasts and YouTube channels you know. Then ask for the holes and approve it.

3

Leave it running

Set the mode to Auto so Claude doesn't stop and wait for you, plug your computer in and ask Claude how to stop it going to sleep. Then go and do something else. My first pass found about 300 quotes. When you've read it, send Claude back for more.

Go againRead what we've got in PROJECTS/ICP/voc. What's thin or missing? Make a plan for a second round of research that goes wider, covers those gaps and avoids sources we've already used.
4

Build your language bank and your short file

Build the filesHere's what I want to end up with: two files I'll use every time I write anything. 1. PROJECTS/ICP/voc/LANGUAGE-BANK.md: every strong quote, grouped by theme, loudest themes first, each with its source link. 2. ABOUT ME/voice-of-customer.md: the short version, under 1,500 words. It should have the rules that came out of the research, a table of words they never use against what they say instead, and the best lines for each theme. Then add voice-of-customer.md to the list of foundation files in CLAUDE.md, tell it to open the language bank whenever I'm writing ads, emails, scripts or posts, and update /weekly-check-in so it covers five files. Where the research disagrees with my ideal-client.md, list it for me and don't change anything. Show me the plan first.
5

Run your clashes again

Your research is new evidence, so go back to Part 4 and settle anything it's thrown up. If the research and your buyers disagree on who they are, go with your buyers.

What To Expect

What Happens What To Do
Reddit, Quora or Facebook block Claude This happened to me. If you want those sites in the research, read the best threads yourself and paste them in.
Quotes get cut short or look slightly off Normal. Most come through a page reader or a machine transcript. See the warning below.
Claude stops partway through You've probably hit your usage limit. Everything's saved, so carry on when it resets.
A lot of it is years old Fine for how people feel, and less so for prices and facts. Ask Claude to mark the date on each quote.
Before a quote goes public

Open the link and check the full sentence is what they said. Use the words to write your copy, and be careful about presenting a stranger's post as a testimonial. Never name anyone from your own calls without asking them.

What the web can't tell you

Research finds what people say to strangers. It won't replace talking to your actual buyers. When Claude tells you what's missing, that's usually your list of people to go and speak to.
