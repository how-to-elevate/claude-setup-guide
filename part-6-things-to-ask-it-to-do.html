How to use a card

Switch to Plan mode, paste the prompt and fill in the brackets. Then follow the six steps from Part 3. Some cards need Claude to get into another app first. Part 3 covers the three ways in, or just ask Claude to walk you through it.

Your Marketing

Write engagement ads

Needs: Parts 4 and 5 done
PromptHere's what I want to end up with: [number] short engagement ads that make my ideal client stop and reply about their own life, to warm up an audience for [what I'm selling or building towards]. Use my foundation files and my language bank, and write in my customers' words. Each ad ends on a genuine question that someone would answer with a sentence about their own life. No asking people to comment, type a word or tag anyone, because Meta punishes that. Tell me how you would do this.

Check before using: every detail about your life has to be true. Claude will fill gaps if you let it.

Build my ads in Meta

Needs: Meta ads connected (see Part 3)
PromptHere's what I want to end up with: these ads built in my Meta ad account, paused and ready for me to review: [point to the file with the copy and images]. I've got the Meta Ads connector, or if I haven't, walk me through connecting it. The official address is https://mcp.facebook.com/ads and it gets added in claude.ai under Connectors. Budget: [amount]. Audience: [who]. Nothing gets switched on or spends money until I've said yes to each ad. Tell me how you would do this.

Check before using: read every ad in Ads Manager before anything goes live. It's your money.

Write an email sequence

Needs: Parts 4 and 5 done
PromptHere's what I want to end up with: a [number]-email sequence that takes someone from [where they are when they join] to [what I want them to do]. Use my foundation files and language bank. Each email needs a real story or detail from my life, so ask me for what you need before you write. Tell me how you would structure it, then wait for my OK before writing any of it.

Check before using: read each one out loud. If you wouldn't say it to a mate, change it.

Your Admin

Clean up my inbox

Needs: a way into your email (see Part 3)
PromptHere's what I want to end up with: an inbox that only shows me the things that need me, with the junk gone. My email accounts are: [list them]. Tell me how you would do this.

Check before using: make sure the plan shows you what's going before anything gets deleted.

Tidy my Google Drive

Needs: Google Drive for desktop or the connector
PromptHere's what I want to end up with: a Google Drive where I can find anything in seconds. Map what's in my Drive first, and don't change anything while you do. Then show me a one-page summary: the folder structure you'd suggest, what's duplicated and what looks like junk. Ask me anything you're not sure about. Nothing gets deleted. Anything that looks like junk goes in a folder called "review before deleting", and I'll decide.

Check before using: shared files and links can break if files move. Ask Claude what could break before you approve.

Keep a daily plan

PromptHere's what I want to end up with: a simple daily to-do list that lives in this folder and that you keep for me. At the end of each day I'll tell you what to do next. You write it under tomorrow's date and carry over anything unfinished. In the first session of each day, you open with that day's list. Set that up, and add the rule to my CLAUDE.md so it happens every time.

Your Knowledge

Turn a call recording into notes and actions

PromptHere's what I want to end up with: this recording turned into a written transcript, plus a short summary of what was decided and a list of who needs to do what. The file is here: [where it is]. Do the transcribing on this computer if you can, so the recording doesn't get uploaded anywhere. If that means installing something, tell me what and why first. Save it all in PROJECTS/[project name].

Check before using: machine transcripts mishear names and numbers. Check anything you're going to act on.

Turn a training platform into your personal coach

Needs: a course you've got access to
PromptHere's what I want to end up with: every lesson from [course name] turned into text in this folder, so I can ask you anything about what it teaches and you'll answer from the training, and tell me which lesson it came from. I've got my own login and this is for my own use. Tell me how you would do this, what you'd need from me, and how long it's likely to take. I'll do any logging in myself.

Check before using: this is for your own learning. Don't share or republish someone else's course.

Bigger Jobs That Come As Skills

A skill is a saved set of instructions that Claude follows every time. Most jobs are fine as a prompt. These two come as skills because they've got programs behind them. You download them once, and then Claude has them for good.

Sort my Google Photos

Being tested Download first

Exports your photos and videos, transcribes every clip and tags them against what your audience says, so Claude can find you the right clip for an ad. It doesn't delete anything from Google Photos. Start with your smallest year.

Download the sort-my-media skill

PromptI've downloaded sort-my-media-skill.zip to my Downloads folder. Unzip it, read HOW-TO-INSTALL.md and install it as a skill for me. Then read WHAT-TO-EXPECT.md and tell me in plain words what's going to happen.

Keep working overnight

Download first

Long jobs can stop when you hit your usage limit. This gets Claude to pick the job back up by itself. Your computer still needs to be on.

Download the overnight skill

PromptI've downloaded overnight-skill.zip to my Downloads folder. Unzip it, read HOW-TO-INSTALL.md and install it as a skill for me. Then tell me how to use it.

Your Own Ideas

Look at your last week. Anything you did twice, put off or hated is a candidate. Describe what you'd like to be true instead, and ask.

When you don't know where to startRead my foundation files. Then ask me about my typical week, one question at a time, and find the jobs I'm doing that you could take off me. Rank them by how much time they'd give me back, and start with the easiest win.
When you keep doing the same jobWe've done this job a few times now. Turn it into a skill so I can run it with one command in future. Show me the plan first.
