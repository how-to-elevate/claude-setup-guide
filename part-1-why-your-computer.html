The Four Ways To Use Claude

Same Claude underneath. What changes is what it can reach.

Quick answers Claude chat

The one you've probably used at claude.ai or in the phone app. It's great for questions and first drafts, and it can connect to things like Gmail and Google Drive. It lives in a conversation though, so your business stays somewhere you can't really see or organise.

Hits limits fast Cowork

Lives in the Claude desktop app. It can connect to other things, but it very quickly meets limits that Claude Code doesn't have.

For developers Claude Code on Anthropic's servers

Claude Code running on Anthropic's computers. It keeps going when your laptop's shut, which sounds perfect until you find out it works from code stored on GitHub, and can't see the files on your computer. You can ignore this one.

This is the one Claude Code on your computer

Claude Code running in the desktop app, in a folder on your own machine. It reads your files, creates new ones, runs programs and uses your connected apps. Every session starts by reading who you are and who you serve. Everything in this workbook happens here.

Why You Want It On Your Computer

Your business ends up in one folder

Your about-me file, your ideal client, your customers' real words, your ads, your scripts, your call notes. It all sits in a folder you can open, copy and back up. Every piece of work adds to it, so Claude gets more useful the longer you use it.

It knows you before you've typed anything

You write one instruction sheet, and Claude reads it at the start of every session. So you never explain your business again, and the writing starts out sounding like you.

It can do the big, boring jobs

This is the bit that surprised me. Mine has transcribed thousands of videos on my own laptop, overnight. You can't do that kind of job in a chat window. When a job needs a program installing, Claude sorts that out too, and asks you first.

It can do things in your other apps

Connect your Gmail, Calendar, Drive or Meta ads account and Claude can work in them from the same place it keeps everything it knows about you. That's how it published my ads.

What It Costs You

Worth knowing

Your computer has to be on. If it goes to sleep, Claude stops. A scheduled job gets skipped if the computer's asleep when it's due. For long jobs, plug in and leave the lid open.

You need a paid plan. The free plan doesn't include Claude Code. Pro is $20 a month, or $17 a month if you pay yearly. If you start running big jobs and hit your limit, you can move up to Max later.

Your files stay on your computer, but what Claude reads goes to Anthropic to be processed. So keep bank details, passwords and anything else you wouldn't email out of that folder.

Nervous about AI seeing all your stuff?

Fair enough. You decide what it can reach, and you start small. Part 3 shows you how.
